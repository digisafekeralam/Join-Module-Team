Be part of the team building a safer digital Kerala. DigiSafe Keralam is focused on creating a safer and more responsible digital society through Cybersecurity Awareness, Digital Well-being, and AI Literacy.

Targeted Modules for Every Audience

Selected students will work alongside experts to create age-appropriate, practical cybersecurity learning modules.

👶

Children

Interactive gaming safety, online privacy basics, cyberbullying awareness, and safe screen habits.

🧑‍💻

Adults

UPI/Payment safety, banking scams, phishing defense, social media security, and family device protection.

👴

Senior Citizens

Digital arrest scam recognition, fake call detection, safe messaging, and simplified fraud prevention.

🛠️ What Will You Work On?

  • 🔹 Identify important cybersecurity awareness topics
  • 🔹 Research real-life cyber risks and scenarios
  • 🔹 Understand the needs of different age groups
  • 🔹 Develop practical and engaging learning content
  • 🔹 Design activities, examples, and assessments
  • 🔹 Review and refine modules with domain experts
